StudioGamalith Studio, Bangladesh

A colour-sorting puzzle game on Google Play. Simple idea, but the feel has to be right or people drop off fast.

What I worked on

  • Built the drag-and-drop controls for moving balls between tubes, with touch input and drop animations.
  • Wrote the move validation rules — same colour on top, empty space check — plus feedback when a move is not allowed.
  • Made a level generator that randomises puzzles and slowly raises difficulty with more colours and tubes.

  • Optimised for low-end Android phones with object pooling and light sprites, so loading is fast and FPS stays stable.
  • Prepared builds and store listing updates and pushed releases through Google Play Console.
